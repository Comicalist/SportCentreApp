## Getting Started

### Welcome to Sport Centre Booking! 🏃‍♂️

This app helps you discover and book exciting sports activities at local centres. Whether you're looking for fitness classes, sports training, or recreational activities, we've got you covered!

### What You Can Do
- ✅ Browse available activities and classes
- ✅ Book activities that interest you
- ✅ Earn and redeem reward points
- ✅ Track your booking history
- ✅ Manage your profile and preferences

### System Requirements
- **Android**: Version 6.0+ (API level 23+)
- **iOS**: Version 12.0+
- **Internet Connection**: Required for booking and real-time updates

## Creating an Account

### 1. Sign Up Process

#### Step 1: Open the App
When you first open the app, you'll see the welcome screen with sign-in options.

#### Step 2: Choose "Sign Up"
Tap the **"Sign Up"** button to create a new account.

#### Step 3: Fill in Your Information
```
📝 Required Information:
├── Full Name (2-40 characters)
├── Email Address (valid format)
├── Password (see requirements below)
└── Confirm Password (must match)
```

#### Step 4: Password Requirements
Your password must include:
- ✅ **At least 8 characters**
- ✅ **1 uppercase letter** (A-Z)
- ✅ **1 lowercase letter** (a-z)
- ✅ **1 number** (0-9)
- ✅ **1 special character** (!@#$%^&*)

**Password Strength Indicator**: As you type, you'll see a visual indicator showing:
- 🔴 **Weak**: Needs improvement
- 🟠 **Medium**: Getting better
- 🔵 **Strong**: Good security
- 🟢 **Very Strong**: Excellent!

#### Step 5: Submit Registration
Tap **"Sign Up"** to create your account.

### 2. Email Verification

#### Automatic Email Sending
After successful registration, a verification email is automatically sent to your email address.

#### Verification Screen
You'll be redirected to the email verification screen which shows:
- 📧 Your email address
- ⏱️ **Auto-checking** every 3 seconds for verification
- 🔄 **Resend email** button (with 60-second cooldown)
- ✅ **Manual check** button
- ⏭️ **Skip for now** option

#### Complete Verification
1. **Check your email inbox** (and spam folder)
2. **Open the verification email** from Firebase
3. **Click "Verify Email Address"** in the email
4. **Return to the app** - it will automatically detect verification

#### Verification Banner
If you skip verification, you'll see an orange banner at the top of the app reminding you to verify your email. Tap **"Verify"** to return to the verification screen anytime.

### 3. Sign In (Existing Users)

#### For Returning Users
1. Tap **"Sign In"** on the welcome screen
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Tap **"Sign In"** to access your account

#### Forgot Password?
1. On the sign-in screen, tap **"Forgot Password?"**
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset instructions
4. Create a new password following the requirements

## Booking Activities

### 1. Simple Booking Process

#### Step 1: Choose Activity
Browse and select an activity that interests you.

#### Step 2: Review Details
Check the activity details, time, location, and price.

#### Step 3: Book Now
Tap the **"Book Now"** button.

#### Step 4: Confirm Booking
Review your booking details and confirm.

### 2. Booking Flow for Guests

If you're not signed in, you'll be prompted to:
1. **Sign In** with existing account
2. **Sign Up** for new account
3. **Continue as Guest** (limited features)

### 3. Booking Confirmation

After successful booking, you'll see:
- ✅ **Confirmation Message**
- 📧 **Email Confirmation** (sent to your email)
- 📅 **Calendar Addition** option
- 🎁 **Points Earned** notification

### 4. Booking Management

#### View Your Bookings
1. Go to **"My Bookings"** tab
2. See **Upcoming** and **Past** bookings
3. Tap any booking for details

#### Cancel a Booking
1. Find the booking in **"My Bookings"**
2. Tap **"Cancel Booking"**
3. Confirm cancellation
4. **Refund** processed according to cancellation policy

#### Modify a Booking
1. Cancel existing booking (if allowed)
2. Book new preferred time slot
3. **Points** and **payment** adjustments applied

### 5. Waitlist System

#### Joining Waitlist
If an activity is full:
1. Tap **"Join Waitlist"**
2. You'll be notified if a spot opens
3. **24-hour window** to accept the spot

#### Waitlist Notifications
- 📱 **Push notification** when spot available
- 📧 **Email backup** notification
- ⏰ **Countdown timer** to accept spot

## Rewards System

### How Points Work

#### Earning Points
You earn points for various activities:
- 🏃 **Completing Activities**: 10-50 points per activity
- 🎯 **First-Time Bookings**: Bonus points for trying new activities
- 📅 **Consistent Participation**: Weekly/monthly streaks
- 👥 **Referrals**: Invite friends and earn points
- 📝 **Reviews**: Points for rating activities

#### Point Values
```
Activity Completion  = 10-50 points
First Visit Bonus   = 25 points
Weekly Streak       = 15 points/week
Monthly Streak      = 100 points/month
Friend Referral     = 50 points
Activity Review     = 5 points
```

### Using Points

#### Discount Application
- 🎁 **100 points** = £1 discount
- 💰 **Minimum redemption**: 50 points (£0.50)
- 🎯 **Maximum per booking**: 50% of activity price

#### Point Redemption Process
1. During booking, see **"Use Points"** option
2. **Slider** to choose points to redeem
3. **Live calculation** of discount
4. **Remaining balance** shown

#### Special Rewards
- 🏆 **500 points**: Free activity voucher
- 🎖️ **1000 points**: Premium membership trial
- 🥇 **2000 points**: Personal training session

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### 🔐 **Authentication Issues**

**Problem**: Can't sign in to account
**Solutions**:
1. Check email/password spelling
2. Use **"Forgot Password"** if needed
3. Clear app data and retry
4. Check internet connection

**Problem**: Not receiving verification email
**Solutions**:
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Wait 5-10 minutes for delivery
3. Use **"Resend Email"** button
4. Verify email address spelling

#### 📱 **App Performance Issues**

**Problem**: App is slow or crashes
**Solutions**:
1. Close and restart the app
2. Restart your device
3. Update to latest app version
4. Clear app cache/data

**Problem**: Can't see latest activities
**Solutions**:
1. Pull down to refresh
2. Check internet connection
3. Log out and back in
4. Update app if available

#### 🎫 **Booking Issues**

**Problem**: Booking button not working
**Solutions**:
1. Ensure you're signed in
2. Check activity availability
3. Verify payment method
4. Contact support

**Problem**: Can't cancel booking
**Solutions**:
1. Check cancellation policy timing
2. Try from different device
3. Contact customer support
4. Use web version if available

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Account & Registration

**Q: Is registration required to use the app?**
A: You can browse activities as a guest, but registration is required to book activities, earn points, and access full features.

**Q: Can I change my email address after registration?**
A: Yes, go to Profile > Edit Profile > Update Email. You'll need to verify the new email address.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password?" link on the sign-in screen. You'll receive an email with reset instructions.

### Bookings & Payments

**Q: When am I charged for a booking?**
A: Payment is processed immediately upon booking confirmation. Refunds follow our cancellation policy.

**Q: Can I book activities for other people?**
A: Currently, each account can only book for the registered user. Family accounts are coming soon.

**Q: What's your cancellation policy?**
A: 
- **24+ hours**: Full refund
- **12-24 hours**: 50% refund
- **Less than 12 hours**: No refund
- **Emergency exceptions** may apply

### Points & Rewards

**Q: Do points expire?**
A: Points remain active as long as you use the app within 12 months. Account inactivity beyond 12 months may result in point expiration.

**Q: Can I transfer points to friends?**
A: Point transfers are not currently available, but you can refer friends to earn bonus points.

**Q: What happens to points if I delete my account?**
A: All points are forfeited when an account is permanently deleted. Consider redeeming points before account deletion.

### Technical Issues

**Q: Which devices are supported?**
A: The app works on Android 6.0+ and iOS 12.0+. Tablet versions are optimized for larger screens.

**Q: Can I use the app offline?**
A: Limited offline viewing of your bookings is available, but internet connection is required for booking activities and real-time updates.

**Q: Is my personal data secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

### Activities & Venues

**Q: How do I know if an activity is suitable for beginners?**
A: Each activity shows skill level requirements. Look for "Beginner Friendly" tags or check the detailed description.

**Q: What should I bring to activities?**
A: Each activity listing includes required equipment. Most venues provide basic equipment, but personal items like water bottles and towels are recommended.

**Q: Can I request new activities or venues?**
A: Yes! Use the "Suggest Activity" feature in the app or contact our support team with your requests.
